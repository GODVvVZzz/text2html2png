# Community code of conduct

We want this project to be a practical, welcoming place for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, specific, and generous in review. Critique the work, not the person. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized language or imagery.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior makes the community unsafe or unproductive. Serious concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
